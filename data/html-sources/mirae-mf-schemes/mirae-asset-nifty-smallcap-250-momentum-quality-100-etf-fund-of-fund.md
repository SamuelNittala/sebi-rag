#### ABOUT THE FUND

[Image: about]

The investment objective of the scheme is to provide long-term capital appreciation from a portfolio investing in units of Mirae Asset Nifty Smallcap 250 Momentum Quality 100 ETF. There is no assurance that the investment objective of the Scheme will be realized.

#### Highlights

*   Investing in growth companies which are in relatively early stage, they offer potential of high growth prospects
*   Exposure to segment/theme which are not adequately present in large & midcap space.
*   The Nifty Smallcap250 Momentum Quality 100 Index aims to track the performance of the small cap stocks which are selected based on the combination of momentum and quality factors.
*   The scheme seeks to capture the performance of quality 100 small cap companies with comparatively better recent performance in terms of risk adjusted market returns.
*   The Nifty Smallcap250 Momentum Quality 100 Index has outperformed Nifty Smallcap250 Index in 16 out of 18 calendar years
