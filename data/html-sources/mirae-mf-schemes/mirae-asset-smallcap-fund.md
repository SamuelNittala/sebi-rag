## Investment objective:

[Image: investment]

The investment objective of the scheme is to generate capital appreciation by investing predominantly in small cap stocks. From time to time, the fund manager will also seek participation in other equity and equity related securities to achieve optimal portfolio construction.

#### Why invest in Mirae Asset Small Cap Fund?

*   Strong Track Record of Mirae Asset Mutual Fund: The Fund house is known for its robust fund management and relatively better and consistent performance across its various Product offerings.
*   Experienced Fund Management Team: Managed by seasoned professionals with a deep understanding of small-cap opportunities and risks.
*   Disciplined Investment Approach: Mirae Asset emphasizes research-driven and disciplined stock selection, which is crucial for small-cap investing.
*   Portfolio Diversification: The fund is designed to create a diversified portfolio of high-potential small-cap companies across sectors.
*   Liquidity Boost: With improved liquidity in the Indian market, small-cap stocks have the potential for better price discovery and growth.
*   Favorable Policy Environment: Supportive government policies for MSMEs (Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises) could provide a tailwind for small-cap companies.
*   Resilient Domestic Demand: Strong domestic consumption and increasing investments in infrastructure can boost small-cap companies that cater to local markets.
