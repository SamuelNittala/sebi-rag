#### About the fund:

[Image: about]

The Scheme will invest in the units of actively managed Arbitrage and Debt Oriented Mutual Fund Schemes as per the above stated asset allocation. The cumulative gross exposure through Units of actively managed Arbitrage and Debt Oriented Mutual Fund Schemes, Money market instruments / debt securities, Instruments and/or units of debt/liquid schemes of domestic Mutual Funds shall not exceed 100% of the net assets of the Scheme.

[Image: investment]

*   The investment objective of the scheme is to provide long-term capital appreciation from a portfolio investing in a mix of units of actively managed debt oriented schemes and actively managed Arbitrage mutual fund schemes.investing in units of debt oriented and arbitrage schemes of mutual fund.
