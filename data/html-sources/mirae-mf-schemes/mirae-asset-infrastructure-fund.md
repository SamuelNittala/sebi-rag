#### ABOUT THE FUND

[Image: about]

*   The Fund seeks to invest in a basket of stocks benefiting either directly or indirectly from infrastructure development in India.
*   The Fund has flexibility to invest across market cap or style in selecting investment opportunities
*   The Fund will seek to maintain a differentiated positioning at a sector and stock level with a view to generate sustainable long-term alpha

Investment strategy stated above may change from time to time without any notice and shall be in accordance with the strategy as mentioned in the Scheme Information Document. Allocations mentioned on this slide are as per our current proposed strategy. This may change in future depending on market opportunities and fund manager views and would be subject to SID limits. The SID limits of market capitalization shall prevail at all points in time.

## Investment objective:

[Image: investment]

The investment objective of the scheme is to generate long term capital appreciation by predominantly investing in equity and equity related instruments of companies that are engaged directly or indirectly or are expected to benefit from the growth and development of the infrastructure sector in India.

There is no assurance that the investment objective of the Scheme will be achieved.

#### Who should invest?

*   Investors looking to invest as part of their Satellite Portfolio & looking at focused exposure to India’s Infrastructure space
*   Investors with an Aggressive Risk Appetite who are comfortable with higher volatility in pursuit of higher returns
*   Investors with a time horizon of 5 to 7 years+ preferably through systematic route (SIP/STP)
