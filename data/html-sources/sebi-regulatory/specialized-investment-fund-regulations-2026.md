# Specialized Investment Fund under the SEBI Mutual Funds Regulations, 2026

## Current regulatory meaning

Under Chapter IX of the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026, a **Specialized Investment Fund (SIF)** is a product launched by a SEBI-registered mutual fund with the Board's approval. The mutual fund does not need to establish a separate trust for its SIF. The normal mutual-fund regulations, guidelines, and circulars continue to apply unless Chapter IX or another SIF-specific rule provides otherwise.

The products launched within a SIF are called **investment strategies**. They follow the launch procedure applicable to mutual-fund schemes and may be open-ended, close-ended, or interval strategies, with subscription and redemption frequency disclosed in the offer document.

## Core investor conditions

A SIF must not accept less than **INR 10 lakh** from a non-accredited investor, measured across all investment strategies of that SIF. Accredited investors are exempt from this minimum. SIF fund managers must hold the relevant NISM certification specified by SEBI.

## Risk and investor protection

SIFs are designed for more specialized investment strategies and are relatively higher risk than conventional mutual-fund products. Their offer documents must highlight the product's high-risk nature so investors can make informed decisions. The AMC must maintain a distinct SIF identity, separate from its regular mutual-fund offerings, while trustees remain responsible for oversight, risk management, investor protection, disclosures, and regulatory compliance.
