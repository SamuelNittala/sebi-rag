## Investment Strategy

The Fund invests in a mix of controlled equity exposure - collar strategy (5-70%), special situations (0-10%), arbitrage (5-70%), and debt (25-35%) aiming to deliver relatively consistent performance across market phases through a differentiated approach. The Fund will be very agile in terms of changing allocation between Collar and Arbitrage strategy dynamically with a view to deliver risk-adjusted returns across market phases. While return enhancement will be sought through Collar Strategy & Special Situations, stability will be sought through Debt & Arbitrage.

## How it works

The Platinum Hybrid Long-Short Fund follows an actively managed, multi-asset strategy that dynamically allocates across equity, debt, and arbitrage opportunities.The fund aims to balance growth and stability by adjusting exposures based on evolving market conditions. The equity portion is managed using controlled exposure strategies, including collar strategies and selective participation in special situations, to capture upside while limiting downside risk. Alongside, arbitrage positions are used to generate relatively stable, market-neutral returns through fully hedged strategies. The debt allocation focuses on high-quality instruments to provide accrual income and reduce overall portfolio volatility. The portfolio is actively monitored and rebalanced to maintain optimal allocation, manage risk efficiently, and enhance return potential across different market cycles.

### What is a Specialized Investment Fund (SIF)?

A Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) is a new asset class introduced by SEBI, positioned between portfolio management services (PMS) and Mutual funds, accepting investments of ₹10 lakh & ₹5 lakh for accredited investor across all investment strategies under the Platinum SIF or more and offering advanced investment strategies.

(\*Across all investment strategies under the Platinum SIF)

### How did Specialized Investment Funds Originate?

SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) introduced Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs) to meet the evolving needs of India’s financial ecosystem.

In India, the concept of Specialized Investment Funds originated as part of the broader effort to promote newer investment alternatives that go beyond traditional mutual funds and combines the advantages of an AIF/PMS.

### Why are bank account details mandatory?

Bank account details are mandatory in Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs) and Mutual Funds to ensure regulatory compliance with SEBI, prevent money laundering, and ensure secure transactions.

In order to protect unit holder's interest from any fraudulent encashment of cheques, the current SEBI Regulations has made it mandatory for investors to mention complete core banking details in their application, such as the bank name and account number of the unit holders.

The AMC will not be responsible for any loss arising out of fraudulent encashment of cheques and or any delay /loss in transit. In the absence of these details, applications are liable for rejection.

### How do investors redeem their units?

Investors may redeem any amount or any number of units, as specified in the redemption request. However, following such redemption, the investor’s aggregate investment across all investment strategies, calculated at the Permanent Account Number (PAN) level, must not fall below ₹10 lakh. If, due to partial withdrawals (and not because of market movement), the aggregate investment falls below ₹10 lakh, the investor will be required to fully exit all investment strategies. Partial redemptions that result in the aggregate investment falling below ₹10 lakh are not permitted.

Redemption request can be made only 2 times in a week (Monday & Thursday) or at any lesser frequency as may be decided by the AMC.

Redemption requests received after Thursday 3.00 PM till Monday 3.00 PM would be considered for processing with Monday NAV, and requests received after Monday 3.00 PM till Thursday 3.00 PM would be processed with Thursday NAV. In case Monday or Thursday is a non- business day, the AMC shall process the redemption on the next business day.

The Trustees reserves the right to change the Redemption frequency in future, subject to SEBI Regulations and any other law, as applicable

### What is the limit of derivatives exposure under SIFs?

SIF investment strategies can maintain unhedged short exposure of up to 25% of net assets to exchange-traded derivatives, beyond hedging and rebalancing purposes

### What is the taxation structure applicable to SIF?

The taxation structure for SIFs will be similar to that of mutual funds

### What happens if my investment value goes below the minimum investment threshold?

AMCs are not allowed to accept investments below ₹10 lakh. However, if the investment value falls below this threshold due to a market decline, it will be considered a passive breach and not treated as a violation. In such a case, the investor will only be permitted to redeem the entire remaining investment from the SIF.

### Does SIF offer the same facilities as SIP, SWP and STP – as in a mutual fund?

Yes. SIP facility is available under the SIF. However, the Strategy minimum amount criteria need to be fulfilled. Investors can opt for SIP of Rs. 50,000 and in multiples of Re.1/- with a frequency of Monthly & Quarterly and minimum of 5 instalments, subject to that the minimum investment amount by an investor should not be less than Rs. 10,00,000/-. Investor shall have the option of choosing any date of the month as the SIP date from 01st to 28th.

The clauses on SWP and STP will be subject to compliance with provisions mentioned under “Minimum investment threshold” as stated under Clause “Minimum threshold requirement and consequences of non- maintenance” of the ISID.

### Is switch allowed within SIF across MF/SIF?

Switch allowed from MF to SIF and within SIF schemes but not from SIF to MF.
